## Supporting answer targets (internal editorial reference)

- **Does statement fashion have to be bold?** No. Small details, quiet colours and meaningful accessories can be expressive.
- **How can I find a personal style?** Observe garments you use, note the details you like and experiment with combinations.
- **Can clothes diagnose personality?** No. Clothing can communicate chosen preferences, not reliably establish personality.
