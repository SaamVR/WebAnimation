### Source creative brief (adapt to V1 override)

Create an original fashion illustration showing four different styling interpretations of the same basic T-shirt silhouette. Each illustrated figure should reinterpret the garment through a different combination of graphics, colours and accessories. Figure 01 — Understated Expression A plain neutral T-shirt, simple trousers and minimal accessories. Figure 02 — Artistic Expression A T-shirt featuring a distinctive original illustration, expressive accessories and relaxed trousers. Figure 03 — Playful Expression A colourful graphic T-shirt combined with unexpected patterns and contrasting colours. Figure 04 — Cultural Expression A T-shirt featuring an original Bengali-inspired motif, complemented by traditional textile details or handcrafted accessories. The figures should have diverse body proportions, individual postures and distinct styling choices. Use confident hand-drawn outlines, visible brushwork, restrained screen-printing textures and subtle imperfections. Maintain a sophisticated editorial illustration style rather than a cartoon or rigid fashion-design template.

The background should be warm cream paper, with Press Green, Terracotta, muted mustard, charcoal and carefully selected brighter accents. Introduce small hand-drawn colour swatches and graphic marks around the figures to emphasize their individual visual palettes. Keep the overall composition airy and balanced.

### V1 creative direction (takes precedence over original brief)

Preserve the source concept, exact ratio, captions and culturally accurate representation. Avoid copying reference layouts or inserting title text into the image.

### Source creative brief (adapt to V1 override)

Create a sophisticated portrait of a young adult Bangladeshi woman wearing a relaxed cream T-shirt with a small original hand-drawn bird illustration positioned subtly on the chest. Pair the garment with dark olive or charcoal relaxed trousers and simple handcrafted silver jewellery. A small maroon teep may introduce a personal cultural detail, presented as an individual styling choice rather than a requirement of Bengali identity. The model should appear comfortable and naturally expressive, with an understated pose and a thoughtful, relaxed expression. Use a simple indoor setting with a warm textured wall, soft natural window light and gentle shadows. The composition should focus on the relationship between the person's expression, the small artistic garment detail and the simplicity of the overall outfit.
Emphasize:
- Realistic cotton fabric texture.
- Natural garment folds.
- A subtle, accurately positioned printed illustration.
- Refined handcrafted accessories.
- Authentic skin texture.
- Soft analogue photographic grain. Use warm cream, Deep Forest, olive and restrained terracotta accents. The image should communicate the idea that personal expression does not require visual excess. Avoid luxury-fashion stereotypes, excessive makeup, exaggerated styling and artificial-looking skin. This is a conceptual generated editorial photograph, not a documentary portrait of a real individual.
