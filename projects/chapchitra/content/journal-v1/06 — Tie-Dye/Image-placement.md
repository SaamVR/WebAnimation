### Source creative brief (adapt to V1 override)

Create a carefully organized illustrated pattern guide featuring four different tie-dye techniques. Arrange the composition in a two-by-two grid. Each panel should show a small illustration of the fabric preparation method beside an unfolded fabric sample demonstrating a plausible resulting pattern. Panel 01 — Spiral Show cotton fabric twisted around a central point and secured with several bands. Beside it, illustrate an unfolded textile with a flowing spiral pattern. Panel 02 — Crumple Show fabric gathered into irregular folds. Beside it, illustrate an unfolded textile featuring organic, cloud-like colour variations. Panel 03 — Stripes Show fabric folded into accordion-style pleats and secured at intervals. Beside it, illustrate a textile with rhythmic bands of colour. Panel 04 — Rings Show sections of fabric individually pinched and bound. Beside them, illustrate circular resist patterns of varying sizes. Use consistent fabric shapes and proportions throughout the four panels. The results should correspond logically to their respective preparation techniques, while acknowledging that actual dye patterns vary. Use expressive ink outlines, subtle paper textures, painted colour fills and delicate fabric-fold details.
Suggested colours:
- Indigo and cream.
- Terracotta and soft rose.
- Forest green and olive.
- Mustard and muted blue.

Keep the backgrounds warm cream, with enough negative space for each technique to remain readable. Add the four technique labels accurately during final design production rather than relying on generated text. The image should serve as a visual introduction to the artistic process, not a complete dye-handling instruction guide.
