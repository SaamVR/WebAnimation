## Supporting answer targets (internal editorial reference)

- **How does tie-dye make patterns?** Binding, folding or twisting protects selected areas from dye.
- **Are bandhani and shibori the same?** No. They are distinct textile traditions sharing resist-dye principles.
- **Is printed tie-dye hand-dyed?** A printed pattern reproduces an image; resist dyeing forms the pattern during dye application.
