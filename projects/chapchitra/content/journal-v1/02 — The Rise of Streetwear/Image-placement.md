### Source creative brief (adapt to V1 override)

Create a cinematic fashion editorial featuring four young adult Bangladeshi models in a contemporary urban environment inspired by Dhaka.
Each model should represent a different streetwear personality:
- A forest-green oversized graphic T-shirt paired with relaxed cargo trousers.
- A cream drop-shoulder T-shirt with wide-leg denim.
- A charcoal graphic T-shirt with a long denim skirt and sneakers.
- A terracotta lightweight overshirt combined with a plain T-shirt and relaxed trousers. The models should have varied body proportions, authentic appearances and individual styling choices. Rather than posing in a straight catalogue lineup, arrange them naturally across the urban environment. One could lean against a wall, another sit on a concrete ledge, while others stand or walk casually.
Include understated architectural details inspired by Bangladesh:
- Textured concrete and brick walls.
- Weathered metal shutters.
- Overhead electrical wires.
- Subtle Bengali shop signage.
- Familiar urban street details. The clothing and models must remain the focal point. Use natural afternoon light, realistic shadows, subtle analogue film grain and sophisticated cinematic colour grading. The overall palette should combine warm neutrals, Press Green, charcoal, Terracotta and muted urban colours. The image should feel youthful, confident and editorial rather than resembling a commercial advertising campaign.

Avoid identifiable third-party logos, meaningless Bengali lettering, exaggerated poses and generic Western urban environments.
