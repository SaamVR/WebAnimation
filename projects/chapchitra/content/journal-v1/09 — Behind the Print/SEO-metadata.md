## Supporting answer targets (internal editorial reference)

- **How does DTG differ from DTF?** DTG prints ink onto the garment; DTF transfers artwork from a film.
- **Is one method always more durable?** No. Materials, preparation, curing and care influence durability.
- **How should printed clothing be washed?** Follow the product care instructions; gentle handling is commonly advised.
