## Supporting answer targets (internal editorial reference)

- **Are oversized and drop-shoulder identical?** No. One describes overall fit and the other shoulder seam placement; a garment may have both.
- **Should I size up a regular shirt?** Compare garment measurements and proportions rather than assuming a larger regular cut fits like an oversized cut.
- **What bottoms work with oversized tees?** Straight denim, cargo trousers, tailored trousers and long skirts are possible combinations.
