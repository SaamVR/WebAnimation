# Shared editorial, layout and search standards — V1

## Editorial

- Use British English, CHAPCHITRA uppercase, accurate Bangladeshi cultural terminology, and no invented firsthand reporting. Titles, decks, body copy and captions are separate fields. Do not append a slogan or signature to every story. No hidden SEO prose or forced FAQ.
- Each article owns its primary intent. The Shop and Artist’s Edit own transactional searches. Keywords in metadata are research guidance, not a meta-keywords tag.
- Author attribution, actual dates, actual canonical domain and publishing decisions require owner confirmation. The source project is a portfolio/demo storefront; do not claim available inventory, pricing, print capabilities or commerce without approval.

## Newspaper-style layout contract

- Follow the supplied editorial references for structure, not copied content or typography. Reference principles: asymmetric feature compositions, oversized live headline, thin rules, mixed image scales, editorial whitespace, selective cutouts and varied columns. The screen recording shows offset images and title blocks with negative space; do not replicate its exact brand.
- Retain existing approved Fraunces display / DM Sans supporting typography and Press Green #293D2D, Terracotta #8A503B, Deep Forest #1C241D and existing cream background. Do not introduce a new global design system. Use a serif display treatment; title remains live semantic H1, not raster text.
- Desktop editorial container: proposed max-width 1360–1440 px with responsive side gutters (24–56 px). Use 12 columns, gap 24–32 px. Feature uses 8 columns (~2/3 editorial content width); title and deck use 4 columns. No 100vw/100vh full-bleed image; no fixed-height hero that clips content. The two columns share the opening spread. Title should be large (suggested clamp(42px, 5.4vw, 86px), line-height ~0.98–1.08) and wrap naturally; do not truncate long headings or shrink all titles to one size. Allow long titles to use more lines. No important text on an image; only intentional decorative silhouette overlap in gutter where text remains readable. Header byline/date/reading time when approved; no fabricated author/date.
- Tablet: use side-by-side only when title and image remain legible; otherwise stack. Mobile (suggested below 768 px): semantic title, subtitle and deck first, feature next, then body. One readable column; no horizontal scroll. Preserve complete image composition. Body copy suggested 16–19 px with 1.55–1.75 line height; aim ~55–75 characters per line. Desktop can use selected two-column text bands or 4/8 asymmetric figure/prose pairs, not tiny dense print-newspaper columns. Tables scroll locally if needed, with accessible header semantics.
- Headline alignment and opening variant are per article in manifest. Supporting slots may be offset, inset or paired with prose; alternate visual scale without rearranging reading order. No repeated identical hero-card template. Use editorial extracts only from actual article text, not invented quotations or attributions; do not duplicate extracts for screen readers.
- Marker `[IMAGE:Jxx-yy]` is a rendering token, never visible to readers. Reserve dimensions and caption region before image load. Use `width`/`height` or CSS `aspect-ratio` as provided; same wrapper geometry for placeholder and final asset. Feature images stay within their grid area. `object-fit: contain` for diagrams and alpha assets; never crop labels, full-body silhouettes or artwork. Any crop of photography requires approval. Preserve stable image IDs, including deliberately non-numeric display order in J02/J04.
- Alpha cutouts: export genuine WebP with alpha; preserve original artboard aspect ratio and intentional negative space; no white matte, checkerboard, rectangular paper background or baked floor. Test on the approved cream background and at mobile scale. Reserve invisible rectangular layout geometry using the artboard. For placeholders, an unobtrusive labelled outline is sufficient during review; final transparent assets render without a card or border. If social metadata requires opaque asset, generate an approved derivative from the same source, not a new story slot.
- Generated imagery is conceptual editorial imagery, not evidence of real artists, historic specimens, manufacturing facilities or available products. Add a concise visible conceptual-image credit/disclosure where appropriate, particularly J07, J08, J09 and J10. Keep captions and alt text accurate to delivered assets. Never represent generated print texture as a real test. Verify Bengali lettering, garment seams, print boundaries, cultural motifs and diagram labels manually. Use actual approved Artist’s Edit screenshot for J08-05.
- Preserve the existing approved Journal/navigation animations and interactions. Developer should use existing motion ownership and integration process; no independent changes to C02 or approved Lottie assets. Do not add forced scroll, fixed-height clipping, artificial loading, automatic page transitions or motion dependencies for content readability. Respect reduced motion. Replacing a placeholder with its matching asset must not trigger layout shifts or require rebuilding animation choreography. Review desktop, tablet and mobile on the existing approved local/non-hosted workflow.

## Search and accessibility

- Render crawlable anchors, one H1, meaningful headings, semantic article/figure/figcaption, table headers, descriptive alt, readable focus states and keyboard-accessible links. Keep source/related links functioning without animation. Resolve all proposed paths against integration owner’s approved routes. Do not use JavaScript-only navigation for links.
- Article/BlogPosting data should match visible content. Use verified headline, description, author, publisher, actual dates, image, section and canonical mainEntityOfPage. Do not fabricate Product/Offer data. No AI-only schema or ranking guarantees. Keep staging non-indexed until authorized public launch; verify indexing policy rather than assuming it is implemented.
- Google SEO starter: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide
- Google crawlable links: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable
- Google AI features: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
- Google generative AI optimization: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide
- Google helpful content: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content

## Production gate

Owner review → local placeholder layout proof → final imagery and asset QA → final source/route/metadata verification → separate authorized release. Do not alter, deploy or create another website as part of this content handoff.
