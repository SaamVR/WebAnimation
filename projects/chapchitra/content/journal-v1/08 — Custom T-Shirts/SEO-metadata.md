## Supporting answer targets (internal editorial reference)

- **What is GSM?** Grams per square metre, a measure of fabric mass per area, not a complete measure of quality.
- **What are A3 and A4?** Standard paper references: A3 29.7 × 42 cm, A4 21 × 29.7 cm. Actual garment print bounds must be confirmed.
- **What resolution is appropriate?** 300 PPI is a useful target for detailed artwork, subject to the provider’s actual requirements.
