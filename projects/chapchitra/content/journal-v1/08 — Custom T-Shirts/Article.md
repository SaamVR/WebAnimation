# The Complete Guide to Custom T-Shirts in Bangladesh

*From Your Imagination to Something You Can Wear*

Discover how to create a custom T-shirt, from choosing the right garment and preparing your artwork to understanding print sizes, placement and printing techniques.

[IMAGE:J08-01]

Have an illustration you would like to wear? The first decision is not which printing machine to use. It is how that artwork should relate to a garment: its shape, colour, size and the way it will be worn.

Creating a custom T-shirt in Bangladesh involves selecting a garment, preparing a suitable artwork file, choosing print dimensions and placement, checking a preview and confirming production requirements with the provider. The following guide helps you prepare those decisions before committing to an order.

## 01. Start with the artwork

Choose a subject and decide what you want the design to communicate. A botanical line drawing, a photograph, an abstract graphic or a piece of original Bengali lettering will each need a different treatment.

Use your own artwork or material you have appropriate permission to reproduce. If you are developing an idea from cultural references, [our folk art feature](/journal/bangladeshi-folk-art-contemporary-fashion/) offers useful context and [Beyond the Graphic](/journal/why-original-t-shirt-designs-matter/) discusses independent artistic interpretation.

[IMAGE:J08-02]

## 02. Choose the garment before the print

- **Regular-fit T-shirt:** a familiar silhouette for smaller or central graphics.
- **Drop-shoulder T-shirt:** a relaxed shape that can complement larger compositions.
- **Full-sleeve T-shirt:** an option for cooler weather or layering.
- **Hoodie:** a heavier garment whose construction, seams and pockets may constrain placement.

These are general garment examples; check available options with the provider. [Fabric weight](https://www.printful.com/uk/blog/t-shirt-weight-guide), measured in grams per square metre (GSM), describes the mass of fabric per unit area, not its overall quality. Composition, knit, finishing and construction also matter. [Our guide to oversized and drop-shoulder T-shirts](/journal/how-to-style-oversized-drop-shoulder-t-shirts/) explains fit in more detail.

[IMAGE:J08-03]

## 03. Prepare a usable file

Start with the highest-quality version of the design. Inspect lettering, thin lines and small details at the intended print size. Remove an unwanted background rather than assuming the printer will do so. A transparent PNG is useful for many digital workflows; vector artwork may be appropriate for other methods. Confirm the actual accepted formats and file limits.

For detailed raster art, 300 pixels per inch (PPI) at the intended print size is a useful target, not a universal minimum. [Printful's print-file guidance](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50264019148177-How-should-I-prepare-my-print-file-for-the-best-results) specifies different resolution recommendations by product. Changing a file's resolution metadata alone cannot recover missing image detail.

## 04. Compare print size and placement

| Size reference | Standard paper dimensions |
|---|---|
| A4 | 21 × 29.7 cm |
| A3 | 29.7 × 42 cm |

A3 has approximately twice the area of A4. These are [standard paper-size references](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~mgk25/iso-paper.html), not guaranteed garment-print limits. The actual permitted region depends on garment size, construction and equipment.

Keep the complete design inside the provider's supported print boundary. Avoid seams, collars and sleeves unless explicitly supported. A small graphic can occupy part of a larger permitted print area; a large print requires enough room for the full composition. If front or back printing is offered, preview the supported sides separately.

[IMAGE:J08-04]

## 05. Confirm the production details

Printing methods differ in fabric compatibility, texture and preparation. [Behind the Print](/journal/how-t-shirt-printing-works/) compares screen printing, DTG and DTF. Before ordering, confirm the actual garment, method, final dimensions, quantity, artwork requirements, total cost and care instructions. Do not assume a method or price from an example in this guide.

## 06. Preview the composition

[Artist's Edit](/artists-edit/) is CHAPCHITRA's creative customisation experience. Use the available controls to explore the relationship between the artwork and garment. The final preview should show the complete design within the supported printing boundary. A digital representation is not a guarantee of exact physical colour, texture or print placement.

[IMAGE:J08-05]

Once your choices are settled, keep the original artwork and the approved preview. They are useful references when discussing the final print with the provider.
