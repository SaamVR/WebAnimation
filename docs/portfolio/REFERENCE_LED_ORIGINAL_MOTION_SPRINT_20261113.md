## Creative gating

1. Produce an original illustrator-grade keyframe/pose sheet before motion coding. Verify silhouette, layer semantics, contours, art consistency, and focal point at mobile resolution.
2. Animate real cause-and-effect with character anticipation/contact/follow-through. No idle floating or opacity-only shortcuts as featured motion.
3. Export actual editable SVG/Lottie JSON; validate lottie-web rendering and mobile/responsive framing. Verify one-use vs looping behavior.
4. Independent critic grades illustration (30%), choreography (30%), product usefulness (20%), presentation (10%), technical integrity (10%). No featured reel animation under 8.8/10 or subscore under 8/10; owner visual acceptance required.
5. Showcase within original fictional product/app contexts; label self-initiated work accurately.
