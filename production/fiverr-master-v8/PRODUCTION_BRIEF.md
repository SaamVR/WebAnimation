## First 10s required checks
- First frame must have a single readable hero at 300px thumbnail width.
- First <0.5 sec needs visible anticipatory motion, not a logo/static billboard.
- No identical framing for consecutive shots, no crossfade ghosts or style mashup.
- First three action payoffs must be observable, not merely announced by labels.
- Alternate object/macro, full scene and functional UI to create contrast without random cuts.
- Five sample frames per scene: start, anticipation, action, resolve, cut-out; evaluate motion in real playback.
- No hardcoded synthetic beeps. Source/license SFX and music; final loudness measured on exported MP4.
- Buyer understands service without captions by 10s; clear supporting copy may come later.
